60-second host script

Read this, in your own words, before round one.

"Welcome to trivia. Tonight we're trying something simple: phones down, game on.

Scan the QR code on your table. You'll earn points for every minute your phone stays down. If you unlock, it shows on the leaderboard — no punishment, just fewer points.

At the end, we draw a raffle from your phone-free minutes. Tonight's prize is from [sponsor name], so thanks to them for putting it up.

Intermission is free time — use your phones then. When we come back, lock in again. Good luck."

After the night, write down

  • Guests who joined: ______
  • Guests who stayed for the raffle: ______
  • Cheating complaints: ______
  • Would you run it again next week? Yes / No / With changes: ______

What not to say

Don't say phones are locked, banned, or monitored. They aren't. Say guests opt in, unlocks are visible, and the raffle rewards staying in the room.
