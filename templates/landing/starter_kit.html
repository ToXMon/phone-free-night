The 10:30pm problem every trivia host knows

You don't need a lecture about screen time. You need a night where a cheating accusation doesn't ruin the room. Today most hosts fix it with pen and paper, walking the floor, and hoping nobody notices the Apple Watch. That works until it doesn't — and it never pays for itself.

Phone-Free Night doesn't replace your questions, your host, or your regulars. It adds three things on top of the night you already run: a reason to keep phones down, a visible leaderboard, and a sponsor-funded reason to stay till the raffle.

How a night runs

1

Set it up in 10 minutes

Create the event, add your sponsor prize, print the table tents. Guests join by QR code in their browser. No app download, no account for guests.

2

Guests lock in, points add up

Guests earn phone-free minutes during rounds. Intermission pauses the clock. Unlocks are visible — social pressure does the work pouches used to do.

3

Sponsors on the leaderboard, raffle at the end

A local brewery, tattoo shop, or burger joint funds a prize and gets named on the leaderboard all night. Draw the raffle weighted by phone-free minutes, with a seed and hash you can show anyone who asks how it was drawn.

What this is not — and why that matters

Not perfect lock proof. Phones and watches buzz. We don't claim to certify a locked phone, and we don't hide unlocks. Not pouches. No hardware to buy, store, or unlock at the door. Not a new trivia app to learn. If you already run trivia, this is scoring, a leaderboard, and a raffle on top of it.

Questions hosts actually ask

Do guests need an app?

No. They open a link in their browser from your QR code.

What if someone just cheats anyway?

They can, and unlocks show up. For a low-stakes raffle, visible unlocks plus a room full of regulars beat a perfect technical lock that doesn't exist on iOS or Android.

Is this only for trivia?

Trivia first, because it's weekly, hosts already pay for software, and cheating is a named pain. Comedy and other live nights come later, through the same towns.

What if my sponsor just hands over a gift card?

List it on the leaderboard anyway. The sponsor still gets named all night, and guests still play for it.
