# Research — Phone-Free Night

Summaries of Ideabrowser verified research, completed 2026-10-28.
Full reports live in Ideabrowser, linked below. Do not treat the napkin
math in these reports as product data: the product did not exist yet when
they were written, and every conversion and sponsor assumption in them
still needs validation with real hosts.

## Easy Night — the product report

- Report: https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/idea-agent/phone-free-event-software-easy-night/report
- Verdict: PIVOT, medium confidence
- Scores: pain 6, timing 8, feasibility 6, opportunity 5
- Pivot: rebrand from "phone-free event software" to a trivia-host SaaS
  that includes phone-free-minute scoring and sponsor raffles as features.
- Price: free tier, plus $19/month for weekly hosts, plus a 15% take rate
  on sponsor prize pools. The old $49/event and $99/month prices sit above
  the trivia-SaaS floor and are not used.
- First customers: bar trivia hosts in r/bartriviahosts, r/triviahosts,
  and the Trivia Hosts Facebook group. Not day one: comedy clubs (staff
  already enforce it), weddings (no budget line, one-off), pro teams.
- Culture signal: Eventbrite says US phone-free event attendance rose
  913% from 2024 to 2025.
- Demand warning: solution-side search demand is 0/month for "phone free
  comedy show", "yondr pouch alternative", and "unplugged wedding app".
  Distribution is community and in-person, not SEO.
- Competitors to beat: Trivnow ($25-$100/month, 500+ events/month),
  TriviaFlow, TriviaRat ($19/month), Trivia Anywhere (free to 25 players).
- GTM: free Starter Kit (table tents, host script, QR raffle page,
  sponsor pitch template), 60 days in the host communities before pitching,
  DM hosts posting about cheating, co-run one free night at 5 local bars,
  film the room, post short-form video, ask for referrals at 20 hosts.
- 90-day napkin (assumptions): ~100 trial hosts, 25 paid at $19/month
  ($5,700 ARR SaaS), plus sponsor pools for a blended ~$21,300 ARR.
  Year-one realistic $30k-$70k. Ceiling $1.5M-$3M ARR.

## Stadium report — the sponsor-pilot alternative

- Report: https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/idea-agent/white-label-fan-engagement-saas/report
- Verdict: PIVOT, medium confidence
- Pivot: drop the Solana badge layer (Socios owns on-chain fan rewards)
  and sell "sponsor activation receipts" to brand sponsorship VPs, not to
  clubs. Lead with an $8k-$15k sponsor-paid pilot, not a club SaaS
  subscription. Verify presence with NFC check-in, Bluetooth beacon dwell,
  or periodic QR scan, not an unprovable "locked minutes" claim.

## Sponsor report — the run-club alternative

- Report: https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/idea-agent/a-sponsor-funded-event-marketplace-phone-free-attention/report
- Verdict: GO, medium confidence, with a pivot in framing
- Direction: run-club-first sponsor rail with a CFO-grade brand-lift
  report, plus a services ladder ($79 playbook, $399 done-with-you) while
  a 25-host roster seasons for the first $2k-$5k brand campaign.
- Use: treat run clubs as a second beachhead test after trivia, not a
  parallel build.

## Brand hallmark used for the landing page

Plain, direct, warm without ceremony. Evidence first, honest caveats
visible, no fake testimonials, no invented customers, no perfect
lock-proof claim. Phone-friendly, fast to load, one clear offer:
the free Starter Kit, then the $19 weekly host plan.
